From text to finished artwork

Make a word cloud in three steps

  1. 01

    Add your text

    Choose a language and enter up to 1 MiB of text. Analysis runs in your browser, and the latest draft is saved in your browser's localStorage.

  2. 02

    Review the words

    Inspect the extracted terms, adjust their counts, and decide which words belong in the design. The editor supports up to 500 unique terms.

  3. 03

    Style and share

    Arrange and style the word cloud, then export your result or share a V snapshot link when you are ready.

Know what each path handles

Your text and shared results

The browser editor and the agent-facing MCP service process text in different ways. Choose the path that fits the text you are working with.

Browser editor

The editor analyzes your source text in the browser and saves the latest draft in browser localStorage.

V snapshot links

A V link contains derived words, counts, ranks, and layout—not the original source text. Anyone who has the link can view those derived values.

MCP text generation

The separate MCP service is unauthenticated. Its wordcloud-generate-from-text tool accepts a sourceText input and sends it to a Worker. It accepts up to 32 KiB of source text and returns up to 100 derived terms, SVG, and V data. Consider whether the text is appropriate to submit to this service before using it.
